# Report Flash Mode - Fast Reading Synthesis

`flash` mode delta. Read this together with `prompts/base.md`; everything in
base still holds. `flash` is for quick orientation, not a shallower copy of the
abstract.

Write a compact report that lets a reader decide how to read the paper next:

- Lead with the paper's core move in plain research language.
- Keep only the method details needed to understand that move.
- Decide which two or three figures/tables carry the main evidence, and embed
  the whole candidate Figure blocks for the ones you discuss. The App stitches
  their source fragments into one image per figure before importing the report;
  do not manually compose or redraw replacement assets.
- Surface the strongest boundary or caveat, even in the short version.
- Prefer flowing prose with one or two concrete headings at most.

Do not force a fixed template. If the paper's own structure is unusual, follow
that structure. The output should feel like a fast but careful colleague saying:
"Here is the idea, here is why it works, here is the evidence, and here is the
part to be careful about."
