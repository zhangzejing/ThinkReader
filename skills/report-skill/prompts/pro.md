# Report Pro Mode — Deeper Synthesis

`pro` mode delta. Read this together with `prompts/base.md`; everything in base
still holds. `pro` goes deeper, not longer for its own sake — keep the report
short and dense.

Add, beyond `auto`:

- **Mechanism depth.** Walk the key equation(s) or algorithm enough that a
  reader understands *why* the method works, not just that it does. Connect the
  math to the result it produces.
- **Evidence reading.** For the central figure/table, say what the comparison
  actually demonstrates and against what baseline — not just the headline
  number. Note ablations or controls that isolate the contribution.
- **Complete figures.** Choose key evidence figures after reading the paper.
  The App stitches every selected multi-fragment Figure using source page
  positions before importing it. Keep the original caption and original
  subfigure labels; do not invent missing labels or redraw scientific content.
  If per-fragment geometry is missing, disclose the source-order stitching
  fallback instead of presenting it as the original layout.
- **Critical stance.** Distinguish what the paper proves from what it asserts.
  Surface the strongest limitation or unstated assumption a careful reviewer
  would raise, grounded in the paper's own setup.
- **Placement.** One or two sentences on where this sits relative to the prior
  work the paper positions against — using only what the paper itself states.

Still short. Density over length: every added sentence earns its place by making
the idea, the evidence, or the boundary sharper.
